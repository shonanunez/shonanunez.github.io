Find your flow

Four ways to take a bubble break.

Poppello brings colourful bubble-popping games to iPhone and iPad, with a companion bubble break on Apple Watch. Choose a game mode to suit your moment.

Bubble Pop

Pop the good bubbles, leave the distractions and work through the levels.

Bubble Snap

Tap when a bubble’s colour matches the previous one and build your score.

Memory Pop

Watch the bubbles light up, then repeat the sequence in the same order.

Endless Pop

Choose your bubble colours and movement, then settle into an open-ended bubble-popping break.

Make it yours with custom colour palettes, and look back on your bubbles popped, sessions and streaks.
